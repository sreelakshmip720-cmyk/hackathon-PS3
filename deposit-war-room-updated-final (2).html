Rule engine

Five evaluation categories, ordinary wear-and-tear exclusion, fixture depreciation capped at 10% per year, and a one-month notice compliance check. These additional evaluation clauses are not claimed as verbatim Karnataka Rent Act provisions; the result is a negotiation anchor, not a statutory damages award.

Legal basis & source trail

This prototype uses the Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 as enacted by Karnataka Act No. 34 of 2001 and reflected in the India Code text, including amendments noted there. Section 47 addresses landlord repairs; Section 48 addresses tenant duties and negligent damage; Section 49 concerns essential supplies/services. The Fifth Schedule allocates specified structural versus day-to-day repairs.
